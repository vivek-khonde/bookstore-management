About BookHive

Welcome to the BookHive, an application designed to help book lovers manage their books, track their reading progress, and discover new books.

Features:

  • Browse and view a collection of available books.
  • Save your favorite books in your personal collection (MyBooks).
  • Edit or delete books in your collection.
  • Admin functionality to manage available books.

Contact Information:

If you have any questions, feel free to reach out to us at support@bookhive.com.

Getting Started:

To start using the system, simply register or log in to your account, and start exploring the available books!
